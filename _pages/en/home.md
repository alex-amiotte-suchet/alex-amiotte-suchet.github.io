Welcome to my academic webpage.

I'm a PhD candidate in economics at [ENS (University) Paris-Saclay](https://ens-paris-saclay.fr/en), and my research is conducted at the [IDHE.S](https://www.idhes.cnrs.fr/) lab.

My research deals with the institutions of SMEs' and innovation's public financing in France. It sits at the intersection of institutionalist political economy, small business and innovation economics, and economic history, and combines archival work, interviews and administrative microdata.

## Featured work

**"Financing businesses: an affair of state? Rise and financialisation of public financing for small and medium-sized enterprises in France"**, *New Political Economy* (2026).

Drawing on 108 archive boxes and ten interviews, this article traces how the French institutional arrangement for public SME financing was built around interwar guarantees, reshaped by the introduction of public loans in the 1980s, and gradually financialised from the late 1990s onwards.

[Published version](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13563467.2026.2683978) · [Open-access preprint](https://hal.science/hal-05330715v2) · [Replication data](/en/data/)
